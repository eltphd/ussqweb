The Credential

From lived experience to conferred credit.

No algorithm issues a transcript at Atlas Academy, and no checkbox does either. Every credit is a human judgment, made by a qualified educator, on evidence your teen actually produced.

I · Portfolio

Students capture real artifacts — field journals, data, film, essays, built things — as they work.

II · Expert review

Credentialed educators assess each portfolio against standards-aligned rubrics and record a written rationale.

III · Transcript

Validated learning is conferred as formal credit on a college-ready transcript, formatted for accreditation-portfolio acceptance.

Transcripts are issued under transparent umbrella-school and portfolio-assessment conventions. Atlas Academy is pursuing accreditation through Cognia; families receive a plain-language explanation of exactly what the credential is at every stage.

Questions families ask

Is the deposit really refundable?

Yes — $500, fully refundable for any reason until enrollment is confirmed. If a season doesn't reach its greenlight of ten committed families, every deposit is returned automatically. The risk stays with us, where it belongs.

Who reviews my teen's work?

Credentialed educators, under the direction of Dr. Erica L. Tartt, PhD — an educational researcher and evaluator. Every credit carries a human sign-off and a written rationale.

What about safety and data?

A safeguarding and disclosure-response policy governs all youth-facing operations. Family data is handled consent-first and FERPA-aligned; nothing is shared or used without explicit written permission, and opting out never affects services.

We're mid-journey — does the schedule bend?

That's the design. Twelve-week seasons, your choice of six; the credential and the peer community travel with you.
