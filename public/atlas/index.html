The Experience

Six weeks that count. In every sense.

A 4-day-per-week academic hub in Costa Rica. Mornings in seminar, afternoons in the field on an SDG capstone with local partners — turtle-nest surveys, regenerative agriculture, community documentation. Evenings belong to the cohort. Everything you make here counts.

Flexibility

Education that bends to your travel schedule.

Each season runs twelve weeks; your family chooses its six. Two overlapping cohorts, capped at 24 students in total, ages 14–18. Arrive from anywhere, leave for anywhere — the credential travels with you, and the Atlas peer community continues online between seasons.

The Credential

From lived experience to conferred credit.

No algorithm issues a transcript here, and no checkbox does either. Every credit is a human judgment, made by a qualified educator, on evidence your teen actually produced.

Portfolio

Students capture real artifacts — field journals, data, film, essays, built things — as they work.

Expert review

Credentialed educators assess each portfolio against standards-aligned rubrics and record their rationale.

Transcript

Validated learning is conferred as formal credit and issued on a college-ready transcript — formatted for accreditation-portfolio acceptance, with Cognia accreditation being actively pursued.

Transcripts are issued under transparent umbrella-school and portfolio-assessment conventions. Atlas Academy is pursuing accreditation through Cognia; families receive a plain-language explanation of exactly what the credential is at every stage.
