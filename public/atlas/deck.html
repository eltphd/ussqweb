05
The Thesis

Bottom-up AI: the season is also the dataset.

Most education AI is trained top-down on generic internet text. Atlas inverts it: every manual credit-mapping decision made by a qualified educator during a season becomes a labeled training example for the Transcript Engine — under explicit consent and data-use agreements. The AI recommends; educators decide; every transcript carries human sign-off. That design is also what accreditors require.

07
Go-to-Market

The revenue ladder — sequence is the strategy.

Stage 1 · Demand

Invitation-only page → discovery calls → $500 refundable deposits. Target 12–24.

Stage 2 · Ground

Deposit-funded Costa Rica scouting: venue LOIs, NGO MOUs, budgets.

Stage 3 · Conversion

Greenlight at 10+ committed students; deposits convert to tuition.

Stage 4 · Season

Run the season, pay salaries, bank profit, collect assessment evidence.

Stage 5 · Scale

Profit + grants fund the AI build; Cognia application follows the season.

No venue contract, hire, or travel until demand is proven. The metric that matters is deposits, not decks.

08
The Moat

Compliance and consent as strategy.

  • Consent-first data architecture — no student data trains anything without explicit written consent; opting out never degrades service.
  • De-identification before training — FERPA-aligned handling; COPPA posture for any under-13 data.
  • Human-in-the-loop by construction — AI recommends, credentialed educators decide, every transcript carries human sign-off.
  • Truth in credentialing — no "accredited" claims until Cognia grants it; transparent umbrella-school conventions until then.
  • Safeguarding in force — existing disclosure-response policy, vetted local staff, international liability coverage.

This is what lets a data-driven education company survive contact with accreditors, regulators — and parents.
